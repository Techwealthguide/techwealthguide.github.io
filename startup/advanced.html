Advanced Startup Strategy

This section focuses on building durable businesses through disciplined execution and intelligent growth.

1. Founder-Market Fit

The best founders are deeply connected to the problem they are solving. Domain knowledge reduces learning curves and increases decision quality.

Founder-market fit often matters more than idea originality.

2. Distribution Before Optimization

Growth fails when distribution is ignored. Channels matter more than features in early stages.

Find one reliable acquisition channel before expanding.

3. Unit Economics and Reality

Profitable growth requires positive unit economics. Vanity metrics hide structural problems.

Understand contribution margins early.

4. Capital Strategy

Capital is a tool, not validation. Raising money changes incentives and constraints.

Many strong businesses are built without external funding.

5. Compounding Execution

Startups succeed through consistent execution, not single breakthroughs. Systems beat heroics over time.

Discipline compounds.

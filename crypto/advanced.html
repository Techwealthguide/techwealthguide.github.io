Advanced Crypto Strategy

This section focuses on protecting capital, understanding market structure, and avoiding common failure points.

1. Self-Custody and Security

Advanced users prioritize ownership over convenience. Self-custody means controlling your private keys and understanding storage methods.

Hardware wallets, multisignature setups, and redundancy reduce single points of failure.

2. Market Structure and Liquidity

Crypto markets are fragmented across exchanges and jurisdictions. Liquidity varies widely, increasing volatility and slippage.

Understanding this structure prevents emotional decision-making.

3. Speculation vs Investment

Most losses occur when speculation is mistaken for investing. Investing requires a thesis, time horizon, and risk controls.

Speculation requires strict position sizing and exit rules.

4. On-Chain Signals and Misinterpretation

On-chain data can be useful, but it is often misused. Context matters more than individual metrics.

Avoid narratives built on single indicators.

5. Long-Term Crypto Exposure

Crypto should be sized appropriately within a broader portfolio. Survivorship bias is real.

Long-term exposure favors simplicity and security over activity.
